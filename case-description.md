# Introduction

You are the lead architect for SW Products in Beumer. Beumer has produced projects for Airport and logistic centres for over 20 years. Products are forked by projects and modified to fit the project tender. Which means there are unique customer specific versions of each product per site. This has served Beumer well, as we have been able to bid on tender's that our competitors are not willing to bid on – because of the high complexity and customizability. But is now starting to bite our tail because the products are expensive to upgrade and maintain over time.

EU is now rolling out new CRA legislation, we are required by law to ensure products delivered on project site to be continually upgraded to be cyber resilient 5 years from delivery. This means that we need to scan our code for vulnerabilities as well as ensure that vulnerabilities found, are patched and rolled out to our customers within a reasonable timeframe. Along other cyber resilience requirements like – secure communication, input validation etc.

This operation is currently very costly as it involves finding the differences between the deployed version of the product and the newest version of the product, while also maintaining the project specific implementations compatibility. This can run up in several thousands of hours to develop and test.

## Task

Make a strategy that ensures we can upgrade the products in our suite on an ongoing basis. While also maintaining the ability to customize our products to fit airport needs.

Have in mind how to technically achieve the strategy, but also on a people change management level how do you ensure you get the developers/architects engaged.

Present your strategy in whatever format you are comfortable with.

Make your own assumptions and state them before your presentation.
